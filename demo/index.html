In the native app

  • Prepare English and Spanish language assets before your visit.
  • Place one phone between you. Each person taps their side and speaks a short turn.
  • The original caption appears first, then the translation. Turns are capped at 12 seconds and history at 12 turns.
  • Clear, returning to setup, or leaving the foreground clears conversation history.
